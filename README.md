```text
• Nghe đọc (Text-to-Speech) từng mục.
• Lưu mục yêu thích để xem lại.
• Giao diện sáng/tối sang trọng, dễ đọc.
• Có hướng dẫn sử dụng ngay trong app.
• Giao diện 8 ngôn ngữ: English · 简体中文 · Español · Português (Brasil) · Русский · 日本語 · 한국어 · Tiếng Việt.
```

## 🇬🇧 English

**App title (≤ 30 chars):** Live Better: Live Smarter

**Short description (≤ 80 chars):** 767 tips on health, time, money & law. Read aloud, currency conversion.

**Full description (2970/4000 chars):**

```text
Live Better — a quick reader of practical steps that help you live longer, waste less time and money, and avoid legal trouble.

The app collects 767 tips across 34 topics, organized in 4 main groups:
• Longevity — safety, screenings, vaccines, first aid, elder care, newborn care, medicine at home…
• Time & Energy — habits, skills worth learning, work, parenting, relaxation…
• Money — avoiding wasted spending, renting/buying a home, starting a business, living on little…
• Personal Freedom & Legal Boundaries — common legal pitfalls, account security, travelling abroad, paperwork after life events…

Every tip is shown concisely: Title · Evidence rating (A/B/C) · In plain words · Cost · Benefit.

FEATURES
• Fresh picks every 5 minutes: the home screen shows one random tip from each of the 4 groups and changes them every 5 minutes — even if the app was closed.
• Instant search that works with or without Vietnamese accents.
• Menu to browse 4 groups → 34 topics.
• Automatic currency conversion: prices in the book are in Chinese yuan; the app converts them to the currency of your chosen language (e.g., 10,000 CNY ≈ 38,540,000 VND or ≈ 1,498.64 USD). Rates are approximate and for reference only.
• Read-aloud (Text-to-Speech) for each tip.
• Save favorites for later.
• Elegant light and dark themes designed for comfortable reading.
• Built-in how-to-use guide.
• Interface in 8 languages: English · 简体中文 · Español · Português (Brasil) · Русский · 日本語 · 한국어 · Tiếng Việt.
• In-app update prompts (downloads in the background; tap restart to install).

PRIVACY
No account needed. Favorites and settings stay on your device. The app shows Google AdMob ads; where required, it asks for your consent before showing ads and you can change your choice in Settings.

IMPORTANT NOTES
• This is a community edition that has not been reviewed by experts. Content is for reference only and is NOT a substitute for advice from a doctor, lawyer or financial advisor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
• The book text is currently available in Vietnamese only; the interface is translated into 8 languages. Many figures and rules come from China or the U.S. — please check the rules where you live.
• Displayed prices are reference conversions from Chinese yuan; real prices vary.
```
